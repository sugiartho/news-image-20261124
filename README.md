front page — Russian jets spark an air-raid alert off Ulleung Island,
Tehran demands $290bn from the US, 26 dead named after the Banten candy
factory fire, Huang returns to Nvidia, El Chapo Isidro to be tried at
home, plus a car bomb in Colombia, the UN's call for AI rules and
Spain's final polls.

The image is drawn from scratch with PIL (`draw.py`, flat vector, no
photos, no copied artwork). `main.ts` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
